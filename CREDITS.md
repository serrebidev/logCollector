# Credits

Log Collector is an NVDA add-on with two parts: it gathers ERROR/CRITICAL entries and tracebacks from the NVDA logs into a copyable report, plus live debug-log capture. Serrebi Fixes are targeted monkeypatches for NVDA core bugs observed in the wild; each fix has its rationale in `globalPlugins/serrebiFixes.py`.

NVDA is NV Access's. This add-on only collects its logs and patches the bugs listed in that file.

Questions and bug reports are welcome in the [SerrebiProjects Telegram group](https://t.me/SerrebiProjects). More serrebi projects: [projects.serrebiradio.com](https://projects.serrebiradio.com).
